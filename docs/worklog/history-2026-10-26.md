**The plan it set:** E1 data signal audit, strict temporal split, baseline ladder, candidate recall, LightGBM vs LLM reranker with a go/no-go gate, then Stage 2 (calibrated purchase probability, richer MILP, inventory simulation, paired bootstrap).
